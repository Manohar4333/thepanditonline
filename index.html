Questions

When is my booking confirmed?

After receiving your WhatsApp request, we’ll check priest availability and discuss the price. Your booking is not final until you receive confirmation.

Who provides the puja materials?

This depends on the ritual. Confirm who will arrange the materials and whether they cost extra before finalizing the booking.

Can I request a Telugu-speaking priest?

Yes, mention your language preference in the request. Availability of a Telugu-speaking priest will need to be confirmed.

How much does a puja cost?

Pricing may vary by ritual, duration, travel, materials and location. Ask for the total cost before confirming.

Can I change my booking date?

Contact us through WhatsApp as early as possible. A date change depends on priest availability for the new date.
